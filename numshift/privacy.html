NumShift — Privacy Policy

Effective: April 1, 2026

TL;DR
Our games collect no personal data. We gather anonymous gameplay stats (scores, levels reached) to improve the game, but nothing is linked to your identity. We don't sell data or show ads.

Who We Are

PapaG Studios is an independent game studio. We build puzzle games for iOS. This policy covers all apps published by PapaG Studios on the Apple App Store.

Data We Collect

Our games do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not know who you are, and we have no way to identify you.

We do collect anonymous gameplay analytics (such as scores, levels reached, and session duration) using Google Firebase Analytics. This data is aggregated and is not linked to your identity in any way. It helps us understand how players interact with the game so we can make it better.

You can opt out of analytics by disabling "Share Analytics" in your iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Data Stored on Your Device

Our games store your preferences (sound, haptics) and local statistics (high score, games played) on your device using iOS standard storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Game Center

Our games may integrate with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. If you are signed into Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center data according to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not access your Apple ID, real name, or any personal details through Game Center.

Third-Party Services

Our games use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous gameplay statistics. Firebase may collect device-level identifiers for analytics purposes, but this data is not used for advertising and is not linked to your personal identity. You can review Firebase's privacy information for details.

Our games do not include any advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.

Children's Privacy

Our games do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Our apps are safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Since we don't collect your email, we can't notify you directly — but the current policy is always available here.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at support at papagstudios.com or visit our support page.
