PapaG TagHunt — Support

Need help with TagHunt? Email support at papagstudios.com and we'll get back to you.

The home screen name is TagHunt; the store name is PapaG TagHunt.

There is no account. Trips stay on your device. Optional TagHunt Plus is a one-time in-app purchase through Apple. Spots and trips can catch up on your other devices via iCloud on the same Apple ID.
