PapaG TagHunt — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 27, 2026

TagHunt keeps your trips on your device. It requires no account, never tracks you across apps, and never sells your data. There is no analytics SDK.

Data stored on your device

The app stores trips, logged plates, and preferences using iOS standard storage. That data does not leave your device unless you choose to buy TagHunt Plus or share a card. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Optional TagHunt Plus is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. Apple may collect purchase history to complete the transaction. We do not link that purchase to an account or identity of our own, and we do not use it for tracking. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

No advertising SDKs. No analytics SDKs. No tracking pixels. No account. No cloud save today.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
