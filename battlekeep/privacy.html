BattleKeep — Privacy Policy

BattleKeep does not collect personal information for its own use, run its own analytics, or sell your data.

BattleKeep shows light ads. Ads are served by a third-party ad network, which may use device identifiers per its own policy; a one-time in-app purchase removes ads entirely. Purchases are handled by Apple — we never see your payment details.

Questions? support at papagstudios.com.

This policy will be finalized with the specific ad-network disclosure before App Store submission.
