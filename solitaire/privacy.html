PapaG Solitaire — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026

PapaG Solitaire keeps your game on your device. It collects no analytics, requires no account, never tracks you across apps, and never sells your data.

Data stored on your device

The app stores preferences (sound, haptics, draw count, layout) and local statistics (wins, best scores, daily results) using iOS standard storage. That data never leaves your device except when you choose to submit a score to Game Center. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Game Center

If you are signed into Apple Game Center, we submit your final Standard score to Apple when you set a new local best on a win. Apple handles Game Center data according to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not access your Apple ID, real name, or other personal details through Game Center.

What we don't do

No advertising SDKs. No analytics SDKs. No tracking pixels. No account. No cloud save.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
