Abide — Privacy Policy

Abide keeps your content — memorized Scripture progress and prayer-journal entries — on your device. It uses anonymous analytics (Google Firebase) to improve the app, but requires no account, never tracks you across apps, and never sells your data.

If any optional cloud sync or backup is added later, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is stored and where, before that feature ships.

Questions? support at papagstudios.com.
