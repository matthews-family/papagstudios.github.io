Abide — Privacy Policy

Abide keeps your content — memorized Scripture progress and prayer-journal entries — on your device. It requires no account, never tracks you across apps, and never sells your data.

Optional anonymous usage analytics (via TelemetryDeck) help us understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Sharing is controlled in Settings → Share anonymous usage data; turn it off anytime and nothing further is sent. Analytics are typed product events only — never verse text, prayers, or notes — and are not linked to your identity. For more detail see TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy and privacy FAQ.

If any optional cloud sync or backup is added later, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is stored and where, before that feature ships.
